MORE THAN A BEAUTIFUL FINISH

A little science.
A whole lot of shine.

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that bonds with your paint. Nanotechnology fills microscopic surface pores, creating a durable, hydrophobic layer that makes everyday care easier.

Long-lasting paint protection

A durable shield against UV rays, oxidation, bird droppings, sap, and light scratches.

Deep gloss. Mirror finish.

A rich, wet-look shine that brings out your paint’s depth and color, long after the detail.

Water beads. Dirt leaves.

Hydrophobic protection helps water, dirt, and grime slide off, making washes easier and faster.

An extra layer of defense

Scratch and chemical resistance helps minimize swirl marks and environmental damage.

Ceramic coating is not scratch-proof and does not prevent rock chips. Regular washing and proper maintenance still matter.
